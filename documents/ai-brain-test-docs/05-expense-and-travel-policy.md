# Expense & Travel Policy

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Owner: Finance (Grace Oyelaran's team).*

## Company card (Ramp)

- All full-time employees above 90 days' tenure are eligible for a Ramp card with a default monthly limit of **$1,500** (higher limits require VP approval).
- Receipts are required for any expense **over $25**; Ramp auto-flags missing receipts after 3 business days.
- Personal expenses on the company card are not permitted, even if reimbursed later — repeated violations go to People Ops.

## Reimbursable categories

| Category | Limit / rule |
|---|---|
| Client meals | Up to $100/person, itemized receipt required |
| Team meals (internal) | Up to $40/person, max once/month per team without VP approval |
| Home office equipment | $500 one-time allowance in first 60 days, plus $150/year refresh |
| Coworking (fully remote employees only) | Up to $300/month, requires manager approval |
| Professional development (courses, books, conferences) | $1,000/year, requires manager pre-approval for anything over $200 |
| Cell phone stipend | $50/month, automatic for roles requiring after-hours availability (on-call Engineering, Support leads) |

## Travel policy

- Book flights and hotels through the Navan travel portal; direct booking outside Navan is reimbursed only up to what Navan would have cost.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for flights over 6 hours with manager approval.
- Hotel budget: up to **$275/night** in major metros (NYC, SF, Chicago), **$180/night** elsewhere.
- Mileage reimbursement for personal vehicle use: IRS standard mileage rate, submitted via Ramp with trip purpose and odometer/mapped distance.
- International travel requires VP approval at least 2 weeks in advance, plus a check with People Ops on visa/work-authorization implications.

## Approval workflow

1. Expenses under $500: manager approval only, via Ramp.
2. Expenses $500–$2,500: manager + Finance approval.
3. Expenses over $2,500: manager + Finance + VP approval, and should ideally be a pre-approved budget line rather than a surprise expense.

## Expense report deadlines

- Submit all expenses within **30 days** of the purchase date. Expenses submitted after 60 days require Finance exception approval and may not be reimbursed.
- Month-end close happens on the 3rd business day of the following month — expenses not submitted by then roll to next month's books.

## Non-reimbursable

Alcohol for solo meals, traffic/parking tickets, first-class upgrades without approval, gym memberships (covered separately under Benefits & Perks), gifts to colleagues, and anything flagged under the Code of Conduct as a conflict of interest.
