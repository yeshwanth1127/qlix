# Frequently Asked Questions

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Pulled together by People Ops from the most common `#ask-hr` and `#it-help` questions.*

**Q: How many PTO days do I get, and can I take them in December?**
A: 18 days/year, accrued monthly. Retail Ops and Warehouse staff can't book PTO between Nov 15–Dec 31 (peak season blackout) except for pre-approved emergencies. See the Employee Handbook, section 2.

**Q: When does my health insurance start?**
A: The 1st of the month following your hire date — not your literal start date. See Benefits & Perks.

**Q: I lost my company laptop. What do I do?**
A: Notify IT immediately via `#it-urgent` or an emergency `NIMOPS` ticket. Do not wait until morning. IT will remote-wipe within 4 hours of notification. See the Data Security Policy and IT Support & Access Rules.

**Q: Can I expense my gym membership?**
A: Yes, up to $50/month as a wellness reimbursement submitted through Ramp, separate from your $300/year wellness stipend.

**Q: How much is the 401(k) match?**
A: 100% of the first 3% you contribute, plus 50% of the next 2% — up to a 4% match if you contribute 5%. It vests immediately.

**Q: I'm relocating to another state. What do I need to do?**
A: Submit a Work Arrangement Change in Rippling before you move. People Ops needs up to 2 weeks to handle payroll tax registration in the new state — moving without notice can cause payroll and compliance problems.

**Q: Do I need approval to work from another country for a couple weeks?**
A: Up to 20 business days/year is fine as a "workation" with 1 week's notice to your manager. Longer or recurring international work needs Legal + People Ops review.

**Q: What's the approval threshold for expenses?**
A: Under $500: manager only. $500–$2,500: manager + Finance. Over $2,500: manager + Finance + VP.

**Q: How do I request access to a production database?**
A: That's Tier 2 access — file an "Access Request" in `NIMOPS` with your manager's approval attached. Standard SLA is 2 business days.

**Q: Who can create or delete AI Brain knowledge collections?**
A: Only users with the org-admin role (Tier 3 access) can manage knowledge collections; regular members can query them if the collection is shared with their agent, per the Data Security Policy and IT Support & Access Rules.

**Q: I'm a new hire starting Monday and don't have a laptop yet — what's going on?**
A: Escalate immediately to your manager and `#it-urgent`. Per the provisioning SLA, laptops should ship to arrive 1 business day before start date; a missed shipment is treated as a priority IT issue, not a "wait and see."

**Q: What happens to my access when I switch teams internally?**
A: Your old team's elevated (Tier 2/3) access is reviewed and typically revoked within 5 business days unless your new manager asks for it to remain.
