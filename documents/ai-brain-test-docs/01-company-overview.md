## Who we are

Nimbus Retail Co. ("Nimbus") is a mid-size direct-to-consumer retail company founded in 2016, selling home goods and outdoor equipment through our website and three physical showrooms (Austin, Denver, Raleigh). We employ approximately 340 people across Retail Operations, Customer Support, Engineering, Marketing, Finance, and People Ops.

## Mission

"Make outfitting your home and your next adventure fast, honest, and low-waste." Nimbus prioritizes durable goods, transparent pricing (no fake discounts), and a 2-year repair-or-replace guarantee on all house-brand products.
