# Employee Handbook — Core Policies

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Effective date: 2026-01-01. Supersedes all prior versions.*

## 1. Working hours

Standard hours are 9:00 AM–6:00 PM local time, Monday–Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Core collaboration hours (when everyone should be reachable) are 11:00 AM–4:00 PM local time. Warehouse and showroom staff follow posted shift schedules instead of standard hours.

## 2. Paid time off (PTO)

- Full-time employees accrue **18 PTO days/year**, accrued monthly (1.5 days/month) starting on hire date.
- Employees may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited on January 31.
- **Blackout period:** PTO requests are not approved for Retail Operations and Warehouse staff between **November 15 and December 31** (peak season), except for pre-approved emergencies.
- PTO requests go through Rippling and require manager approval at least **5 business days** in advance (48 hours for sick leave).
- Unused PTO is paid out at termination in states where required by law (including Texas is not required by law, so no payout applies to TX-based employees; check with People Ops for your state).

## 3. Sick leave

10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is required after **3 consecutive sick days**.

## 4. Parental leave

- Birthing parent: 12 weeks paid.
- Non-birthing parent / adoption / foster placement: 6 weeks paid.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth/placement date and can be split into two blocks with People Ops approval.

## 5. Code of conduct

- Treat colleagues, vendors, and customers with respect; harassment or discrimination of any kind is grounds for immediate termination.
- No alcohol on premises except at company-sanctioned events (max 2 drinks, cash bar or hosted, never during working hours).
- Conflicts of interest (e.g., a manager dating a direct report, or accepting vendor gifts over $75) must be disclosed to People Ops within 5 business days.
- Confidential information (customer data, unreleased pricing, financials) may not be shared outside the company. See the Data Security Policy for classification levels.

## 6. Dress code

Business casual in HQ and showrooms during customer-facing hours; casual dress is fine for back-office and remote work. Steel-toe boots and hi-vis vests are mandatory in the warehouse at all times — this is a safety requirement, not a style guideline.

## 7. Performance reviews

- Formal reviews occur twice a year: **mid-year (July)** and **annual (January)**.
- Ratings scale: Exceeds / Meets / Partially Meets / Does Not Meet expectations.
- Compensation changes are only made at the January cycle, except for promotions or market corrections approved by a VP.

## 8. Progressive discipline

Standard path is: verbal warning → written warning → final written warning → termination. Serious misconduct (safety violations, harassment, theft, falsifying records) can bypass steps and lead to immediate termination, at People Ops' discretion in consultation with the employee's VP.

## 9. Termination & offboarding

- Voluntary resignation requires **2 weeks' notice** (4 weeks for managers and above) to be eligible for rehire consideration.
- IT access is revoked within 1 hour of the offboarding meeting per the IT Support & Access Rules document.
- Final paycheck is issued per state law (Texas: by the next regular payday).
