# Benefits & Perks

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Owner: People Ops. Applies to full-time employees (30+ hrs/week); some items are pro-rated for part-time.*

## Health insurance

- Medical, dental, and vision through Aetna, three plan tiers (Base, Plus, Premium).
- Nimbus covers **90% of employee premium** and **60% of dependent premium** for the Base plan; employees pay the difference to upgrade tiers.
- Coverage starts on the **1st of the month following** hire date (not day 1 — plan ahead if your start date is late in a month).
- Open enrollment: every November, effective the following January 1. Qualifying life events (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage) allow off-cycle changes within 30 days of the event.

## Retirement

- 401(k) through Guideline, eligible after 30 days of employment.
- Company match: 100% of the first 3% contributed, 50% of the next 2% (effectively up to 4% match on a 5% contribution).
- Match vests immediately — no vesting schedule.

## Equity

- New hires above L4 (senior IC) or manager level receive an equity grant, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff.
- Refresh grants are considered annually at the January performance cycle for high performers.

## Wellness

- $50/month gym or fitness-app reimbursement (submitted via Ramp, category "Wellness").
- 1 mental health day per quarter, separate from PTO/sick leave — no approval needed beyond notifying your manager same-day.
- Annual $300 "wellness stipend" usable for things like massage, meditation apps, ergonomic equipment not covered under the home-office allowance.

## Time off beyond PTO

- 10 paid company holidays/year (New Year's Day, MLK Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving + the Friday after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day).
- Volunteer time off: 2 paid days/year for approved volunteering (submit through Rippling, "VTO" category).
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family, 2 days for extended family.
- Jury duty: fully paid, no cap, just provide the summons to People Ops.

## Discounts & perks

- 40% employee discount on all Nimbus house-brand products; 20% on third-party brands carried in showrooms.
- One free "gear loan" per quarter (borrow outdoor equipment for personal use, e.g. tents, kayaks) subject to warehouse availability.
- Annual team offsite budget: $150/employee for a department-organized outing (dinner, escape room, etc.), separate from the company-wide holiday party.

## Parental & family

See the Employee Handbook (parental leave section) for paid leave details. Additionally: a $2,000 fertility/adoption assistance benefit is available after 1 year of tenure, administered confidentially through Carrot.
