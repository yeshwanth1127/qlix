# New Hire Onboarding Flow

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Owner: People Ops (Sam Torres's team).*

## Before day 1 (Recruiting + People Ops)

1. Offer signed in Rippling → triggers background check (Checkr), typically 3–5 business days.
2. People Ops creates the employee record in Rippling and assigns a **buddy** (a peer from the new hire's team, not their manager).
3. IT provisions accounts 3 business days before start date:
   - Google Workspace account (`firstname.lastname@nimbus-retail.example`)
   - Slack, Jira (`NIMOPS` project access), Ramp card request
   - Laptop shipped via IT asset system, pre-imaged with standard software bundle
4. Manager schedules a 30-minute "Week 1 map" call for the new hire's first afternoon.

## Day 1

- **9:00 AM** — Welcome session with People Ops (group, all new hires that week): benefits enrollment, I-9/W-4 paperwork, handbook acknowledgment e-signature.
- **11:00 AM** — Laptop + account setup with IT; MFA enrollment is mandatory before any system access is granted.
- **1:00 PM** — Meet your buddy; office/warehouse tour or remote-equivalent virtual tour.
- **2:30 PM** — 1:1 with manager: review 30/60/90 plan draft (see below).
- End of day: complete required compliance training modules in Rippling Learning (Code of Conduct, Data Security Basics, Anti-Harassment) — due within first 5 business days.

## Week 1

- Shadow at least 2 team members in adjacent roles.
- Meet with a cross-functional partner (e.g., Engineering new hires meet Customer Support; Support new hires meet Engineering) to understand how the product is used end to end.
- Access checklist confirmed complete by IT (see IT Support & Access Rules, section "New hire provisioning SLA").

## 30/60/90 plan

- **Day 30:** Ramping — complete role-specific onboarding curriculum, ship or complete one small, low-risk task/project.
- **Day 60:** Contributing — own a defined piece of work with light supervision; first informal check-in with skip-level manager.
- **Day 90:** Independent — full ownership of assigned scope; formal 90-day review with manager, documented in Rippling Performance.

## Roles with additional onboarding steps

- **Warehouse & Fulfillment:** OSHA safety orientation (in person, 2 hours) and equipment certification (pallet jack, forklift if applicable) before floor access is granted. No exceptions, including for visiting engineers doing "gemba walks."
- **Engineering:** Production access (AWS, database read replicas) is granted only after the Data Security Basics training is marked complete AND a manager approves the access request ticket in `NIMOPS`.
- **Customer Support:** Zendesk shadow queue for first 2 weeks; cannot send customer-facing replies solo until passing the support QA calibration quiz (80% or higher).

## Offboarding trigger note

If a new hire's start date is rescheduled or the offer falls through, People Ops must notify IT within 24 hours to cancel account provisioning — do not let pre-created accounts sit active with no owner.
