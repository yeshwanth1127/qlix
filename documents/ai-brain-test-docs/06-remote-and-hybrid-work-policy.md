# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

*Fictional test content for Nimbus Retail Co. Owner: People Ops, co-signed by department VPs.*

## Work location tiers

- **HQ-based (Austin):** Expected in-office Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Monday/Friday flexible remote.
- **Hybrid-satellite (Denver, Raleigh showroom staff):** In showroom per posted shift schedule; back-office days can be remote.
- **Fully remote (approved roles only):** Primarily Engineering, Customer Support (non-showroom), and Marketing individual contributors. Requires VP sign-off recorded in Rippling.
- **Warehouse & Fulfillment:** On-site only; not eligible for remote work given the role.

## Requesting a remote-work arrangement

1. Employee submits a request through Rippling ("Work Arrangement Change").
2. Manager reviews within 5 business days considering team coverage and role requirements.
3. VP approval required for any fully-remote arrangement or a move to a different state/country (tax and legal implications).
4. People Ops confirms compliance (payroll tax registration, labor law posting requirements) before the arrangement is finalized — this can take up to 2 weeks for a new state.

## Working from abroad

- Employees may work from another country for up to **20 business days per year** without a formal arrangement change, as a "workation," with manager notice at least 1 week ahead.
- Anything longer, or working from abroad as a standing arrangement, requires Legal + People Ops review for tax/immigration compliance — this is not guaranteed to be approved.

## Equipment & expenses

See the Expense & Travel Policy for the home office allowance ($500 initial, $150/year refresh) and coworking stipend ($300/month for fully remote employees).

## Meeting norms for hybrid teams

- Default to "one person remote, everyone remote" for video calls — if even one attendee is dialing in, all attendees join from their own laptop/camera rather than clustering around a conference room mic, to keep the meeting equitable.
- Meetings should have a written agenda shared at least 30 minutes in advance; no-agenda recurring meetings get reviewed for cancellation each quarter.
- Async-first for anything that doesn't need real-time discussion — prefer a Slack thread or a doc comment over scheduling a call.

## Time zone coverage

Customer Support must maintain live coverage 7:00 AM–9:00 PM Central Time, 7 days/week. Remote Support employees outside Central Time must have at least 5 hours of overlap with these hours in their local schedule.
